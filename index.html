QUALIFICATION OVERVIEW

What is HNDE Qualification?

NVQ Level 6 Standard

Equivalent to a Higher National Diploma benchmarked against international engineering technician standards.

Governed by SLIATE

Sri Lanka Institute of Advanced Technological Education under the Ministry of Education Sri Lanka.

IIESL Recognized

Graduates qualify for associate membership in the Institution of Incorporated Engineers, Sri Lanka.

Industry Driven Curriculum

Designed with 50% theoretical concepts and 50% practical workstation and laboratory skills.

ACADEMIC BRANCHING

Year 2: Specialization Selection

Branching Criteria

At the beginning of Year 2, students specialize based on 1st Year SGPA (Semester Grade Point Average) and Student Preference.

Electrical Engineering

Focuses on Power Systems, High Voltage, Generation, Smart Grids, and Electrical Machines.

OR

Electronic & Telecom Eng.

Focuses on Microelectronics, Wireless Communications, Fiber Optics, Signal Processing, and IoT.

STREAM 1

Specialization: Electrical Power Engineering

Prepares engineers to design, operate, and maintain heavy current energy infrastructure:

Power Generation & Transmission

Hydro, thermal, solar power, and grid sub-station protection.

Electrical Machines & Drives

Transformers, AC/DC motors, generators, and motor control centers.

Renewable Energy & Smart Grids

Solar PV systems, wind turbines, energy auditing, and conservation.

STREAM 2

Specialization: Electronic & Telecom Eng.

Focuses on micro-current systems, communication protocols, and modern electronics:

Embedded Systems & Microcontrollers

Arduino, ARM processors, PCB design, and IoT programming.

Telecommunication Networks

4G/5G mobile networks, fiber optical communications, and RF.

Digital Signal Processing (DSP)

Audio/video processing, filter design, and computer networks.

Electrical vs Electronics Engineering

Understanding the key differences and core fields of electrical and electronic engineering:

High Voltage Infrastructure

Understanding grid distribution, circuit breakers, power transformers, and switchgear protection systems.

Low Voltage & Embedded Systems

Designing micro-controllers, circuit boards, signal processors, and IoT devices.

Grid & Communication Reliability

Focuses on fault analysis, power stability, and integrating telecommunication networks.

ACADEMIC STANDARDS

Evaluation & Academic Regulations

80% Mandatory Attendance Rule

Minimum 80% attendance is required in lectures and practical labs to qualify for semester examinations.

Continuous Assessment (40%) & End Semester Exam (60%)

Grades are evaluated through quizzes, lab reports, mid-terms, and final written exams.

SGPA / GPA Credit System

Maintaining a high SGPA is crucial for Year 2 stream selection and degree transfer pathways.
